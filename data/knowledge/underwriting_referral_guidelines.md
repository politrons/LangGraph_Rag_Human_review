# Underwriting referral guideline

## Property risk

Refer a property submission to a senior underwriter when the model risk score is at
least 0.70, the requested exposure exceeds the delegated authority limit, or the
submission contains incomplete material risk information. An automated service may
recommend a referral but must not bind coverage.

## Evidence and traceability

Every recommendation must cite the policy version and the evidence chunk used. A
human reviewer must approve, reject, or escalate a recommendation before a case is
routed to a downstream case-management system.
